# GoreeCloud

**GoreeCloud is my personal hobby project, not a business.**

I build GoreeCloud for the enjoyment of creating technology I own and control. It is my privacy-first, self-hosted personal and family cloud ecosystem, and a place to develop original applications, explore ambitious ideas, learn, and experiment at my own pace.

I want GoreeCloud to be sophisticated, powerful, polished, and innovative. None of that requires it to generate revenue, become a company, or justify itself financially.

## Why I build GoreeCloud

### Enjoyment and creativity

I build because I want to. Curiosity, imagination, and the satisfaction of making something useful are reasons enough to create. I want this project to stay fun, personal, and creatively rewarding.

### Ownership and independence

I prioritize self-hosting, open-source software, interoperable technologies, data portability, and meaningful control over my infrastructure and applications. Where third-party technology is useful, I want to choose it deliberately, respect its licensing, and avoid unnecessary vendor lock-in.

### Minimal recurring expenses

I prefer affordable, maintainable solutions that minimize unnecessary subscriptions, service fees, and ongoing commitments. I evaluate costs according to personal value and long-term sustainability, not potential business returns.

### Quality without commercial pressure

I want to create polished, accessible, secure, reliable software and improve it at a sustainable pace. I do not need artificial shipping deadlines, sales targets, investors, growth metrics, or commercial expectations to take engineering quality seriously.

### Learning and experimentation

GoreeCloud is a space to try difficult things, explore new technologies, make discoveries, refine ideas, and learn from mistakes. An experiment can be worthwhile even if it never becomes a finished product or earns money.

## What success means to me

Success means building an ecosystem that reflects my vision and serves my personal and family needs, while remaining practical for me to own, understand, operate, and maintain.

I can take pride in an elegant design, a reliable application, a useful integration, better privacy, or a lesson learned—without asking whether any of it has a financial return.

**GoreeCloud can be sophisticated, powerful, and innovative without ever making a dollar.**

**The goal is to build the ecosystem I envision, not turn my hobby into another job.**

## Development direction

I intend to develop original GoreeCloud applications and cohesive experiences across the ecosystem while using appropriately licensed libraries, frameworks, protocols, and other technical foundations when they are beneficial.

I favor long-term control, privacy, security, maintainability, portability, and thoughtful integration. These are guiding intentions, not claims that every planned capability is already implemented or production-ready.

---

*GoreeCloud is a personal, noncommercial hobby and learning project. Its scope and pace are determined by interest, usefulness, available time, and responsible stewardship—not a business model.*
